milky way trip - Alvord desert, new moon weekend (Jun 14-16)

GEAR
- body: the old Nikon D750 + backup batteries x4 (cold at night drains them)
- 14-24mm f/2.8 — main lens for sky
- 24-70 for daytime / playa
- sturdy tripod (NOT the travel one, wind out there is brutal)
- intervalometer for timelapse
- red headlamp (white light ruins everyone's night vision)
- lens warmer strip + usb battery — dew on the lens killed half my shots at Crater Lake
- 2 extra SD cards, 128gb

SETTINGS TO START WITH (from the forum)
- manual focus on a bright star, live view zoomed 10x, tape the focus ring
- f/2.8, ISO 3200, 20 sec (500 rule: 500/14 ≈ 35s max but 20s keeps stars pin sharp)
- RAW, white balance ~3900K
- long exposure noise reduction OFF (takes forever), shoot dark frames instead
- for star trails: 30s, stack 200+ frames

SHOT LIST
[ ] galactic core rising over the Steens ridge (core up around 11:30pm, best ~1-2am)
[ ] the hot springs w/ steam + stars (light paint the steam a tiny bit)
[ ] car + tent under the milky way, someone holding a lantern
[ ] star trails from the playa, circle around Polaris (point north!)

[ ] daytime: cracked playa textures, sunset on the mountain
[ ] timelapse from sunset into night
