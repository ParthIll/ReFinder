# garden 2026

zone 7b. last frost ~Apr 10, first frost ~Oct 25

## what went wrong last year
- tomatoes got blight by August — too crowded, watered from above
- zucchini: squash vine borers killed all 3 plants in July
- lettuce bolted immediately in June
- the carrots were like little fingers. soil too compacted?

## plan: 3 raised beds (4x8) + pots on the patio

**Bed 1 — tomatoes & friends**
- 4 tomato plants ONLY (not 7!!): 2 Sungold cherry, 1 Cherokee Purple, 1 San Marzano for sauce
- 30" apart, sturdy cages + prune suckers
- basil tucked between them, marigolds along the front edge (pest thing, maybe placebo, they're pretty anyway)
- drip line under mulch, NO overhead watering

**Bed 2 — leafy + roots**
- spring: lettuce, spinach, radishes, peas on the trellis (direct sow mid-March)
- summer: swap to bush beans + Swiss chard (heat tolerant)
- carrots in the corner with sand mixed in — loosen to 12"
- fall: garlic planted in October for next year

**Bed 3 — squash & cucumbers**
- try zucchini again but wrap the base of the stems in foil, and cover with row cover until flowers appear
- cucumbers on a cattle panel arch between bed 2 and 3
- nasturtiums

**Patio pots**
- herbs: rosemary, thyme, mint (always in its own pot, it takes over), parsley, chives
- 2 pepper plants (jalapeño, shishito)
- strawberries in the hanging planter

## seed starting indoors (under shop lights in the basement)
- Feb 20: peppers
- Mar 1: tomatoes, basil
- Mar 25: cucumbers, zucchini (they grow fast, don't start early)
- harden off the week of Apr 5

## soil
- top up each bed w/ 2 bags compost
- soil test from extension office ($15) — pH was 6.1 last time, fine
- mulch with straw after planting
