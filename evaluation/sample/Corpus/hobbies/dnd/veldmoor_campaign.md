# The Drowned Lanterns of Veldmoor
### campaign bible — DM EYES ONLY (looking at you, Theo)

Levels 3–8, roughly 15–20 sessions. 5e, mostly rules-as-written but see house rules at the bottom.

---

## The pitch (what I told the players)

Veldmoor is a fishing town at the edge of a vast marsh, built on stilts and connected by rope bridges. For as long as anyone can remember, the townsfolk have hung lanterns over the water on the longest night of the year to "light the way home" for the drowned. This year, the lanterns lit themselves — and the next morning, six children were missing. The party arrives on a supply barge three days later, hired by the reeve to find them.

## The truth (what's actually going on)

Two hundred years ago, Veldmoor was a prosperous trading city on dry land. Its ruling council made a bargain with a marsh spirit, the **Pale Mother**, to hold back the floods in exchange for a "tithe" every generation. When the council stopped paying, the Pale Mother drowned the city. The current town is built on top of the sunken ruins.

The Pale Mother has been slowly waking, and a small cult in town — **the Keepers of the Wick** — wants to restore the old bargain. They believe if they deliver the tithe (the children) on the night of the next new moon, the drowned city will rise again and they'll rule it.

**The cult leader is Marrow Hesk, the friendly old innkeeper of the Gutted Eel**, where the party will almost certainly stay. He is warm, generous, gives the party free stew, and quietly feeds them misinformation. He is 74 years old and his own granddaughter was "tithed" fifty years ago; he genuinely believes she'll come back when the city rises. He should be likable. When the reveal happens, I want the players to feel bad about it.

DO NOT let Hesk be too helpful early on or they'll get suspicious. He should give one piece of real, useful information for every misleading one.

## Major NPCs

**Reeve Ilsabet Corran** — the town's leader. Tired, practical, distrusts outsiders but is desperate. Her nephew Bran is among the missing. Not involved with the cult. She will pay 300 gp for the children's return, and 100 gp more if they find "who did this."

**Marrow Hesk** — see above. Tells: always smells faintly of lamp oil; has a burn scar on his palm in the shape of a spiral (the cult mark); gets very quiet when the drowned city is mentioned. Stats: use Cult Fanatic with +20 HP and the ability to cast *fog cloud* at will.

**Sister Dunla** — a priestess of the sea god who runs the small chapel. Red herring #1: she is secretive and sneaks out at night, but she's actually smuggling medicine to a quarantined family on the outer stilts. If the party follows her, they find the family and gain her trust (and access to her healing potions at cost).

**Yoss the ferryman** — a half-orc who rows people across the marsh. He knows the old channels and is the only person who can guide the party to the sunken bell tower. He's terrified of the Pale Mother and charges triple to go near the Deeps. If the party is kind to his dog, Pickle, he charges normal price.

**Garrow Fenn** — a disgraced scholar who's been mapping the ruins. Red herring #2: he has stolen artifacts from the sunken city in his room, which looks suspicious, but he's just a looter. He has the **Map of the Lower Streets** which the party needs for Act 3.

**The Pale Mother** — the marsh spirit. Appears as a tall woman made of mist and weeds with a lantern where her heart should be. She is not evil exactly; she is a force of nature that was betrayed. A clever party might renegotiate the bargain instead of fighting her (see Endings).

## Act 1 — The Town on Stilts (sessions 1–4)

Goals: investigate the disappearances, meet NPCs, find the first clue pointing at the drowned city.

- **The Lantern Night aftermath:** all the lanterns are still burning, even in rain. They can't be put out by water. A *detect magic* reveals faint necromancy.
- **The missing children's homes:** each family found a wet footprint trail leading to the water's edge and a single unlit wick on the windowsill.
- **Encounter:** while investigating at night on the rope bridges, the party is attacked by 4 **bog-drowned** (zombie stats, but they can submerge and reappear anywhere in the water within 30 ft).
- **Clue:** one wick has a tiny spiral burned into the wax — the same spiral as on Hesk's palm. Make sure this is only noticed on a successful DC 15 Investigation, and only mention it in passing.
- **Sister Dunla subplot** (optional).

End of Act 1: Yoss reveals that on Lantern Night he saw lights *under* the water, near the old bell tower.

## Act 2 — The Marsh (sessions 5–10)

Goals: travel through the marsh, find out about the Pale Mother and the old bargain.

**Random marsh encounters (d8):**
1. Swarm of stirges
2. Giant frog x2
3. Will-o'-wisp pretending to be a lost child calling for help (nasty one)
4. Abandoned fishing boat with a journal from a cultist who changed their mind
5. Lizardfolk hunting party — can be negotiated with; they hate the "lantern people"
6. Quicksand pit (DC 13 Athletics)
7. Ghostly procession of drowned townsfolk — non-hostile, can be questioned once with *speak with dead*
8. Nothing, but the fog thickens and everyone loses track of time

**Key location: The Sunken Bell Tower.** The top three floors stick out of the water. Inside is a mural depicting the council's bargain with the Pale Mother. The bell itself, if rung, summons her (DON'T let them do this in Act 2 unless they really insist; if they do, she appears, speaks, and leaves — she is not ready yet).

**The Lizardfolk Elder, Ssarith,** knows the old story and the most important rule: *the bargain can only be remade by someone who speaks for the town.* (This is the hook for the Reeve in the endings.)

**Mid-campaign twist (session ~8):** the party returns to the Gutted Eel and Hesk has prepared a feast for them. The stew is drugged (DC 14 Con save or poisoned for 1 hour). That night, cultists try to steal any artifacts the party found. If the party catches a cultist, he'll break under interrogation and say "the old man will bring them all home" — but won't say who.

The real magic item reward in Act 2 is the **Lantern of the Last Light**: a hooded lantern that, when lit, reveals invisible creatures and lets the bearer breathe underwater for 1 hour (1/day). It is in the bell tower's belfry, guarded by a **water weird**.

## Act 3 — The Drowned City (sessions 11–16)

Goals: enter the sunken city, rescue the children, confront Hesk and the Pale Mother.

- With Garrow's map and the Lantern, the party can enter the city through the flooded **Merchants' Gate**.
- The streets are underwater but the old council hall is in an air pocket. The children are there, asleep in a magical trance, surrounded by candles.
- **Hesk's reveal:** Hesk is waiting in the council hall with 6 cultists. He doesn't attack right away. He tells his story about his granddaughter. He asks the party to leave. Give them a chance to talk him down (DC 18 Persuasion, advantage if they bring up anything he said earlier at the inn about missing her).
- If combat happens: Hesk, 6 cultists, and the council hall slowly floods (each round, roll d6: on a 1–2 the water rises 1 ft).

## Endings

1. **Fight the Pale Mother:** very hard (she's basically a CR 9 elemental with lair actions). If she's defeated, the marsh begins to drain over years and the town will eventually have to move.
2. **Remake the bargain:** if the Reeve comes with the party and speaks for the town, the Pale Mother will accept a different tithe — the town tends the marsh, plants the reed beds, and lights the lanterns honestly every year. Best ending. The children wake.
3. **Hesk's way:** if the party fails, the city rises, and things get weird. Possible sequel campaign.

## House rules
- Potions as a bonus action
- Inspiration given for great roleplay, can be passed to another player
- Crits: max one die + roll the rest
- Flanking off
- Marsh travel: exhaustion check every 8 hrs w/o rest (DC 12 Con)
