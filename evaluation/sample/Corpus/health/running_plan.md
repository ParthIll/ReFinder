# Half marathon plan — Riverbend Half, May 17

goal: finish under 2:10 (last year 2:24, walked a lot of mile 11-12)
current: ~15 mi/week, long run 6 mi

## key paces (from the 5k time trial, 27:40)
- easy: 11:15–11:45 /mi — should be able to talk in full sentences
- tempo: 9:35 /mi
- goal race pace: 9:50 /mi
- intervals: 8:50 /mi

## weekly structure
- **Tue**: workout (tempo or intervals)
- **Wed**: strength (lower body + core) — short
- **Thu**: easy run
- **Sat**: long run, slow!!
- **Sun**: easy 3 or rest
- Mon/Fri: rest or bike

## rules for myself
- 80% of runs EASY. the easy runs are supposed to feel too slow
- don't make up missed runs, just move on
- if knee hurts (sharp, not achy) -> stop, walk home, ice
- new shoes by week 4 (current pair has ~420 mi)
- practice race fuel on long runs >9 mi: gel at mile 5 and 9, sip water every aid station
- race morning: oatmeal + banana 3 hrs before, coffee, nothing new!!!

## race day
- start conservative — first 3 mi at 10:05, NOT faster even if I feel great
- miles 4–10 at 9:50
- last 5k: whatever's left
