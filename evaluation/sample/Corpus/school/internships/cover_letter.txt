Dear Ms. Okonkwo and the hiring committee,

I'm writing to apply for the Summer Operations & Data Intern position at Second Harvest Valley Food Network. I'm a sophomore studying computer science with a minor in nonprofit studies, and I've spent the last two years volunteering at the St. Brigid's pantry on Saturday mornings, so I have seen firsthand how much of a food bank's work depends on getting the right food to the right place before it spoils.

At St. Brigid's, I noticed that we regularly ran out of fresh produce within the first hour while canned goods piled up in the back. I started keeping a simple spreadsheet of what came in on each truck and how quickly each category went out. After a couple of months, the coordinator used it to ask your network for a different mix of deliveries, and we now give out produce for most of the morning instead of the first forty minutes. It was a small project, but it taught me that careful record-keeping can directly change what families take home.

Your posting mentions helping partner agencies report their distribution numbers and improving the routing of the refrigerated truck. I'd be excited to work on both. I'm comfortable with spreadsheets and basic data analysis from my coursework, and I'm used to explaining numbers to people who are busy and don't want a lecture — the pantry volunteers are mostly retirees who will tell you very directly when something is confusing.

I also grew up helping in my grandmother's bakery, where I learned to show up early, lift heavy boxes without complaining, and be friendly to every customer even at 6am. I'm happy to work warehouse shifts as well as desk work.

I'm available from May 20 through August 23 and can work the full 30 hours per week. Thank you for considering my application. I'd love the chance to talk about how I could help.
