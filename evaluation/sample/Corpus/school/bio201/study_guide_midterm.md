## Cellular respiration (BIG section, ~40% of exam per Dr. Patel)

**Overall:** glucose + O2 -> CO2 + H2O + ATP

1. **Glycolysis** - cytoplasm
   - 1 glucose -> 2 pyruvate
   - net 2 ATP, 2 NADH
   - doesn't need oxygen!
2. **Pyruvate oxidation** - pyruvate -> acetyl-CoA, releases CO2
3. **Krebs / citric acid cycle** - mitochondrial matrix
   - turns twice per glucose
   - makes NADH, FADH2, a little ATP (GTP), CO2
4. **Electron transport chain + oxidative phosphorylation** - inner membrane
   - NADH/FADH2 drop electrons off, pumps H+ into intermembrane space
   - H+ flows back through ATP synthase = most of the ATP (~26-28)
   - O2 is the final electron acceptor -> water

total ~30-32 ATP per glucose (textbook used to say 36-38, she said either is fine if we explain)

## Fermentation
- no O2 -> regenerate NAD+ so glycolysis can keep going
- lactic acid (muscles, yogurt bacteria) vs alcoholic (yeast -> ethanol + CO2)

## Enzymes
- lower activation energy, don't change delta G
- competitive inhibitor binds active site; noncompetitive binds elsewhere (allosteric)
- denature at high temp/wrong pH

## Things I keep getting wrong
- ETC is in the INNER membrane, not outer!!
- Krebs makes more NADH than ATP directly
- photosynthesis is NOT on this exam (thank god)
