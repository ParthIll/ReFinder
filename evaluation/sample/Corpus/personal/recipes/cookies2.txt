Grandma Ruth's oatmeal cookies
(copied off the stained index card in her recipe tin, Thanksgiving)

These are the thin crispy ones that snap. NOT chewy. She'd always have a tin of them on top of the fridge.

1 cup shortening (she used Crisco, I've done half butter and it's fine but spreads more)
1 cup white sugar
1/2 cup brown sugar
2 tbsp molasses  <- the secret, gives that dark color
1 egg
1 tsp vanilla
1 1/2 cups flour
1 tsp baking soda
1/2 tsp salt
1 tsp cinnamon
1/4 tsp nutmeg
3 cups quick oats (the quick kind, not old fashioned)
1 cup raisins, soaked in hot water 10 min and drained
(optional: 1/2 cup chopped walnuts — Uncle Pete is allergic so leave out at holidays)

Cream shortening and sugars. Add molasses, egg, vanilla. Mix dry stuff separately then add. Stir in oats and raisins. Dough will be stiff.

Roll into walnut-sized balls, flatten with the bottom of a glass dipped in sugar. 2 inches apart, they spread a lot.

350° for 12-14 min till dark golden all over. Cool on the sheet 2 min then rack. They crisp up as they cool.

Keeps a week in a tin. Grandma's note at the bottom of the card: "Don't skimp on the nutmeg. Hide some from your brother."

doubled it for the cookie swap in Dec — took 4 sheet pans. worth it.
