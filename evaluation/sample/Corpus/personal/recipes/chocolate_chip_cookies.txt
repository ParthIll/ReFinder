THE cookies (brown butter version)
adapted from like 4 different blogs + trial and error. makes ~20

1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter
1 1/4 cup dark brown sugar, packed
1/2 cup white sugar
2 eggs + 1 extra yolk (room temp!!)
2 tsp vanilla
2 1/4 cups AP flour
1 tsp baking soda
1 tsp kosher salt
~10 oz dark chocolate, chopped (bars, not chips — chips don't puddle the same)
flaky salt for the top

1. brown the butter: melt in a light-colored pan on medium, keep swirling. it foams, then gets quiet and smells like toffee and little brown bits appear on the bottom. pour into a big bowl IMMEDIATELY incl the brown bits. let cool 15 min (should be warm, not hot)
2. whisk in both sugars hard for a minute. add eggs + yolk + vanilla, whisk till glossy
3. fold in flour, soda, salt. then chocolate
4. CHILL THE DOUGH. minimum 2 hrs, overnight is best. (tried skipping it once, they spread into one giant pancake)
5. oven 375. scoop big balls (~3 tbsp), 6 per sheet
6. bake 10-11 min — edges golden, middles look underdone. bang the pan on the counter twice when you pull it out, makes the crinkly rings
7. flaky salt on top while warm

notes
- dark brown sugar + extra yolk = chewy. if you want cakey use all white sugar, but why
- mom says they're "too rich." mom is wrong
- freeze dough balls, bake from frozen +2 min
- brought these to Tasha's party and 3 ppl asked for the recipe
