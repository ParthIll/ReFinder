# banana bread

for when there are 3 sad black bananas on the counter again

## ingredients
- 3 very ripe bananas (the uglier the better) — about 1.5 cups mashed
- 1/3 cup melted butter
- 1/2 cup sour cream or plain greek yogurt
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 tsp baking soda
- pinch of salt
- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- optional: handful of walnuts or chocolate chips, or both if it's been that kind of week

## steps
1. oven 350, grease a 9x5 loaf pan (or line w/ parchment sling, way easier to get out)
2. mash bananas in a bowl with a fork, leave some lumps
3. stir in melted butter, then sour cream, sugar, egg, vanilla
4. sprinkle baking soda + salt over, stir. add flour + cinnamon, mix JUST until no dry streaks. overmixing = rubbery
5. fold in nuts/chips
6. pour into pan. optional: slice a banana lengthwise and lay on top, sprinkle with sugar — looks fancy
7. bake 55-65 min. toothpick in the middle should come out with a few crumbs. tent with foil at 40 min if top is getting too dark
8. cool 10 min in pan then out onto a rack. try to wait before cutting (I never do)

## notes
- sour cream is what makes it moist, don't skip
- freezes great in slices, wrap individually, toast from frozen
- muffin version: 12 cups, 22-25 min
- tried with oat flour once for Lena (gluten thing) — denser but good
