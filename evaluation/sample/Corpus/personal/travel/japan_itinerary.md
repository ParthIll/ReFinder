- **Apr 5**: Ueno park for hanami (bring a tarp + konbini snacks). afternoon: Yanaka old town
- **Apr 6**: Shibuya + Harajuku. Meiji shrine in the morning before crowds. dinner reservation at the yakitori place under the tracks in Yurakucho (booked, 7:30)
- **Apr 7**: day trip to Nikko? or Kamakura? -> leaning Kamakura (big Buddha, beach, Enoshima)
- **Apr 8**: teamLab Planets (tix bought, 10am slot), Tsukiji outer market for lunch, pack

## Kyoto (Apr 9-12) — ryokan near Gion, 2 nights w/ kaiseki dinner, then airbnb
- **Apr 9**: shinkansen ~2h15. Philosopher's Path in the afternoon (peak blossom hopefully)
- **Apr 10**: Fushimi Inari at 7am (go EARLY), Tofuku-ji, afternoon tea ceremony class (booked)
- **Apr 11**: Arashiyama bamboo + monkey park. Saga Toriimoto old street
- **Apr 12**: Nara day trip, deer + Todai-ji. don't hold paper near the deer
