trip plan - sept, 9 nights
me + Lena + her parents for the first half

FLIGHTS
out: Boston -> Lisbon overnight, arrive Sep 12 morning
back: Porto -> Boston Sep 21, 11:40am

LISBON (Sep 12-16) - apartment in Alfama, 4th floor NO elevator (warn her dad)
12 - nap. sunset at Miradouro da Senhora do Monte. dinner at a tasca nearby, no reservation needed
13 - tram 28 early before it's packed, Sé cathedral, Castelo de São Jorge. evening: fado show (booked, small place, 9pm, dinner included)
14 - Belém: tower, monastery, and the custard tart bakery (line moves fast). LX Factory in the afternoon
15 - day trip to Sintra by train. Pena Palace tix for 9:30am entry. Quinta da Regaleira well. back by 6
16 - parents fly home. we do Time Out market + pack

PORTO (Sep 16-21) - train from Santa Apolónia ~3h, 1st class is barely more $
17 - Ribeira waterfront, cross the Dom Luís bridge on the top deck, port cellar tour in Gaia (Taylor's? Graham's?)
18 - Livraria Lello (book online, timed entry), Clérigos tower, São Bento station tiles
19 - Douro valley day tour - wine tasting at 2 quintas + boat ride + lunch. booked thru the hotel, pickup 8:30
20 - beach day at Foz, francesinha for dinner (giant meat sandwich covered in sauce, need to try once)
21 - fly home

NOTES
- euros, cards widely accepted but keep cash for tascas
- lots of hills + cobblestones = good shoes
- dinner is LATE, 8:30-9pm normal
- learn: obrigado/obrigada, bom dia, a conta por favor
- budget roughly €2,600 for the 2 of us excluding flights
- travel insurance - did we buy it?? check
